**EQLS Auras 1.0 — buff timers on top of EverQuest Legends**

EQLS Auras reads your EQ log and puts a countdown timer for every buff you're running
on top of the game.

- Every buff you're running, counting down, on screen
- Build as many overlays as you want — position, size, sounds, all yours
- Point an aura at any line in your log, not just buffs the app knows
- Tracks your weekly raid lockouts, which EQ never prints

Windows, free. https://eqlsource.com/tools/
