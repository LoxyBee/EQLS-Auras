Bundled starter sounds - provenance and license
================================================

Two sources are mixed in this folder, both fine to ship in the installer with
no attribution required and no per-file license to track:

1. Chime.wav, Soft Ping.wav, Alert.wav, Bell.wav, Klaxon.wav
   Hand-synthesized by tools/generate-bundled-sounds.js (sine tones, no
   external audio). Not copyrightable third-party content.

2. Confirmation Chime.ogg, Confirmation Chime 2.ogg, Error Tone.ogg,
   Error Tone 2.ogg, Bong.ogg, Glass Ping.ogg, Pluck.ogg, Notify Tone.ogg,
   Select Click.ogg, Soft Tick.ogg
   From Kenney's "Interface Sounds" pack (https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds),
   renamed from their original filenames (confirmation_001.ogg -> "Confirmation
   Chime.ogg", etc.) for readability in the in-app sound picker; audio content
   unmodified. License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain) -
   http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Attribution is not required, but Kenney asks for a credit where convenient:
   "Sound effects by Kenney (kenney.nl)".
